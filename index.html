Given the content HTML of a Mastodon status as its own light-DOM children, this component rewrites hashtag links to point at a local tag-url instead of the origin server, and marks a hashtag-only bottom line as a tag-bar of pills.

It is currently unstyled, and adds classes and wrappers you can style yourself.

See it in action at Ayo's Threads page.

Just shipped a new release, thanks for all the feedback along the way!
